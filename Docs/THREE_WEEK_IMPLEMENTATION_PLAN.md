# Three week implementation plan

Planning window: 5-25 October 2026. Role names below are proposed workstreams, not claims that a named team member has agreed to own them. The four-person team should allocate owners and one reviewer per workstream at kickoff. The desired outcome is an evidence-backed Track A submission, not a photorealistic binary spacetime renderer. This plan is conditional on prompt access to a headset, AI service/backend, study participants and the marking rubric.

## Kickoff decisions on 5 October

Confirm headset and deployment target (standalone versus PC-tethered), compatible OpenXR/XRI packages, physical device access and performance budget. Obtain PI6/PI9 rubrics and Moodle deadlines; the course brief says week 9 submission while Project 05 says week 10 demo. Recover the Context research/evidence register and raw anonymized research before reusing handoff evidence IDs. Select AI provider/service, permitted data handling and institution-approved consent procedure. Book expert review and participant sessions now. Freeze optional redshift, time dilation, NR optics, ringdown and asset packs until all mandatory gates pass.

## Week 1 5 to 11 October

Model and data workstream: move or package the tested portable model so Unity imports it without duplicating equations. One SimulationSession supplies immutable state to both bodies, readouts, frequency graph, scenario controls and tutor. Keep double-precision SI internally and one documented render mapping. Two equal-mass presets vary only initial separation; choose a clear comparison convention. Use analytic state evaluation rather than phase integration tied to render frames. Stop physical frequency at the declared cutoff; conceptual merger/remnant remains a separate visual state.

Learning and research workstream: goal-to-task wireflow, baseline questions, predict/change-one-variable/compare/explain/review sequence, simple and optional detailed wording. Recover evidence, interview the client/expert and varied target users as permitted. Create an affinity diagram, evidence-linked personas/journey and short iteration rationale. Map CAMIL/load management and human-AI principles to actual mechanics; do not infer effect sizes from unrelated studies.

XR workstream: select hardware immediately and import pinned compatible packages in an isolated branch/worktree if approved; verify eye/camera hierarchy, tracked wrist attachment, world-space ray UI, recenter, floor height and seated reach. Keep pose emulation as the same-interface test harness. Do not retain desktop free flight as the default real-headset movement.

AI workstream: agree snapshot schema, version/epoch, units, assumptions, current layer and comparison state; implement external service adapter without credentials in the Unity client. Define cancel, timeout, offline/error and stale-reply states. Questions must be answered from captured model state and approved lesson material, not guessed HUD values.

Gate 1 evidence:

- Unity body positions, separation, frequency readouts and graph agree with portable fixtures at 0%, intermediate times and cutoff.
- r decreases/f rises; fGW=2forbit in the disclosed model; same time gives same state across seek and playback rates/frame rates.
- Layer/camera changes do not change physics; presets pause/reset consistently; invalid input does not corrupt the session.
- Expert records review of approximation, cutoff and mappings; issues have owners.
- Headset smoke build shows tracked views and readable wrist controls, or escalation recorded by day 3.
- Study protocol, recruitment, research traceability and rubric mapping are ready, not deferred until week 3.

## Week 2 12 to 18 October

XR/data interaction workstream: implement tracked grab and bounded two-hand scale/rotate of an abstract frequency/chirp object, not the physical model. Add reset placement, seated/stationary mode, safe optional teleport/snap turn, readable adjustable panels, clear affordances and macro-to-micro reveal/filter controls. Test both eyes: stereo macros alone do not fix the mono-camera star projection. Disable or explicitly label optional optics in headset builds until that rendering is validated.

AI workstream: functional student questions with state-grounded explanations; concise response plus optional detail. Show provider/model status, uncertainty and limits. Add reviewable explanation history, explicit loading/cancel/retry, stale-context rejection after resets/scenario/seek and safe network failure. Captions/text are always available; if speech is introduced, use one audio channel and user-controlled mute. Never log credentials or sensitive voice/text without consent. AI must not perform arbitrary code/tool actions or silently modify model state.

Learning workstream: add baseline and unassisted post-task questions that distinguish closer/faster/higher-frequency and the twice-orbital-frequency relation. Keep formative feedback separate from post-test scoring. Add an expert-level explanation task to reduce ceilings. Build a researcher-only static-chirp comparison condition with matched presentation/time and explicit audit metadata; normal exhibit graph always remains linked.

Validation workstream: run a small pilot on real hardware using think-aloud tasks, including wrist discovery, compare one variable, move/reveal layers, pause/exit and tutor recovery. Use findings to validate or revise the three proposed extras: short guided tasks/exploration, seated adjustable comfort, reviewable tutor/caption history. Capture observation severity, performance and sickness/stop signals; revise and retest.

Gate 2 evidence:

- Real device grab/scale/filter and wrist controls work seated and standing; reset placement/recenter recover reliably.
- All five shaders render without errors in target stereo mode; UI legibility, frame CPU/GPU and thermals/memory are measured on selected hardware.
- Tutor passes an expert-reviewed question suite, including unsupported merger/redshift claims, unit questions, stale snapshots, timeout and cancellation. Offline placeholder is never represented as functional AI.
- Pilot observations, prototype revisions and design-principle-to-mechanic links are saved; required extras have an evidence basis.
- Console/build/runtime diagnostics are reviewed, with mandatory defects corrected before recruiting the main study.

## Week 3 19 to 25 October

Research workstream: run consented target-user sessions, proposed feasible target 8-12 adults with varied prior knowledge/interest (not a guaranteed sample or adequately powered efficacy trial). Counterbalance linked/static chirp order or use carefully allocated groups according to expert-reviewed protocol; use parallel question forms and matched exposure. Include unassisted transfer explanation and qualitative confusion/comfort/trust observations. Record prior knowledge and condition order; separate missing/withdrawn data. Avoid causal claims unsupported by the small design. Analyse paired/per-condition descriptive results, uncertainty, ceilings/order effects and coded themes; triangulate expert review and think-aloud findings.

Engineering workstream: fix high-severity findings, regression-test model/UI/AI/device paths and repeat critical tasks with new users when feasible. Profile worst-case layers, stars and tutor UI on hardware. Preserve the tiny procedural asset strategy; lower MSAA/mesh/optics quality only after measurements while maintaining legibility. Produce clean reproducible target build, package/source provenance and scene/run guide.

Report and portfolio workstream: complete all 11 course chapters: title, management summary, AI statement, introduction, theory, methods, results (research/design/validation), discussion/conclusions, recommendations, enriched APA references, appendices. Include research question answers from observed results, limitations, rejected designs, contribution/feedback/decision logs, prompt/model evidence and anonymized data. Verify each reference's discovery method, study design/participants and relevance. Record <=15-minute demo showing causal model, XR manipulation, layers, AI and limitation/failure handling. Keep PI9 individual evidence separate from technical test output.

Gate 3 evidence:

- Every mandatory audit row links to implementation and/or actual human/documentary evidence; assess against the supplied rubric, not a guessed score.
- Supported headset build and clean install run on the selected target; regressions, errors/warnings and AI safety cases are resolved or explicitly disclosed to the assessor.
- Human results exist; no fabricated learning gains, participant quotes or professional contributions.
- Final report/reference annotations/appendices, demo <=15 minutes and individual portfolio are reviewed by a peer and checked against Moodle submission requirements.
- If any mandatory gate fails, label the submission partial and prioritise the failed requirement over optional graphical/relativistic features.

## Beyond baseline quality

Stronger-than-minimum work means traceable design decisions, expert-validated physical honesty, real tracked data manipulation, robust state-grounded AI failure handling, accessible layered explanations, measured on-device performance and a credible small evaluation with transparent limits. More realistic disks, NR datasets or complex effects do not compensate for missing research or functional requirements. Completion/grade depends on evidence and the actual marking rubric.
